About NPTI

Personal Trainer School: The NPTI Program

According to NPTI:

◈

500-hour diploma certification program, including 200 hours of hands-on lab and a 100-hour nutritionist certification.

◈

Licensed by the Department of Education.

◈

Lifetime credential — no CECs needed.

◈

Hybrid or virtual programs, with new classes every 6 weeks.

◈

330+ campus locations in 20+ countries.

◈

Academic partner with NASM — optional NASM-CPT with Certificate of Distinction.

For enrollment, tuition, and class dates, contact NPTI directly: nationalpti.org · 1-800-960-6294.
