Before Certification · Through NPTI

NPTI Instructor: Hands-On Teaching For NPTI Students

I'm a contracted instructor for the National Personal Training Institute (NPTI). I teach NPTI students hands-on, in person, in San Antonio, alongside NPTI's classroom learning, as they work toward becoming personal trainers.

I teach through NPTI, not on my own. Enrollment, tuition, admissions, class dates, and credentials are all handled by NPTI, and I don't speak for NPTI beyond my instructor role. If you want to become a personal trainer, start by enrolling through NPTI.

About the NPTI program, according to NPTI:

◈

500-hour diploma certification program, including 200 hours of hands-on lab and a 100-hour nutritionist certification.

◈

Licensed by the Department of Education.

◈

Lifetime credential — no CECs needed.

◈

Hybrid or virtual programs, with new classes every 6 weeks.

◈

330+ campus locations in 20+ countries.

◈

Academic partner with NASM — optional NASM-CPT with Certificate of Distinction.

Want to enroll? Contact NPTI directly: nationalpti.org · 1-800-960-6294.

After Certification · With Me Directly

Trainer Mentorship: Put Your Certification To Work

NPTI teaches the curriculum. I teach the floor — using my own app, programming, and assessment methods in real sessions.

My mentorship runs on my own system: the Bodies By Jake mobile app, my programming, and my assessment style, taught through live, hands-on reps, not lectures. It's my system, not a resale of NPTI's.

You learned it from the book. Now learn to do it with real people. My mentorship is for trainers who are already certified and want real-world application of what they studied.

Here's how it works: I bring you on as my client, but the purpose is education. You follow along with my programming in live sessions while I teach you what I'm doing and why. It's classroom-style learning built into real training.

01

Conducting assessments. How to run an assessment and turn the results into a plan.

02

Identifying issues. How to spot movement problems and limitations, and what to do about them.

03

Coaching real people, hands-on. Cueing, correcting form, and adjusting a session in real time.

04

The business of training. What it takes to build and run a coaching business.
